How to install an update
  1. Download and extract the release ZIP.
  2. Replace the contents of your existing extension folder with the new extension folder's contents. Keep the same folder location.
  3. Open your browser's Extensions page and click Reload on IPTUBE. Refresh YouTube.

Your saved settings stay in place. Do not remove the extension. Chrome requires this manual step for unpacked extensions.
